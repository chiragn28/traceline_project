Business case

Know who touched the data.
Prove it in minutes, not days.

A read-only audit-trail layer for MENA fintech and trading firms — for compliance officers, CTOs, DPOs, and auditors who currently can't answer "who accessed this, and why" without days of manual work.

TRACELINE
01 / 09
01 — The problem

An auditor asks one question nobody can answer cleanly.

"Show me everyone who accessed customer PII last quarter, and confirm it was authorized." Today that means database logs, a stale spreadsheet, and a Slack thread — days of work, and an answer nobody fully trusts.

Compliance officer — signs an attestation they can't verify CTO — engineers pulled off product work DPO — no system built to answer residency questions Auditor — stuck accepting manual, self-reported evidence

02 / 09
02 — Why now

Three forces just made this urgent.

01Regulatory tightening across UAE/GCC financial regulators on data governance and access controls.
02More firms exist that need this — DIFC and ADGM fintech/trading-firm licensing has grown well past "figure it out later" scale.
03Enterprise buyers now require it — SOC 2 / ISO 27001 certification is becoming a condition of the deal itself, not just the audit.
